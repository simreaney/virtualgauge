    How to read it

    index = 100 × (latest level − lowest level) ÷ (highest level − lowest level)

    Each reach is compared with its own record of water levels from the SWOT satellite. The index says where the river stands between its lowest and highest recorded level. It is not a depth of water and does not show whether a particular boat can pass.

    Levels: SWOT L2 HR RiverSP reach product, Version D (NASA/CNES, PO.DAAC), through Hydrocron. River network: SWORD v17b. Made with virtualgauge.
